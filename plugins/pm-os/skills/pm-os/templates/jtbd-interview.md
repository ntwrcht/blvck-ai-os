## Ground Rules
- Past behavior only — "tell me about the last time…", never "would you…"
- Follow the timeline: first thought → passive looking → active looking → deciding → first use
- Silence is a tool; let them finish

## Opening (5 min)
1. Tell me about your role and what a normal week looks like.
2. Walk me through the last time you [did the job / hit the problem].

## Timeline Dig (25 min)
3. When did you first realize you needed something? What triggered it?
4. What did you try before? What was wrong with it? (pushes: what did it cost you — time, money, standing?)
5. Who else was involved in the decision? What did they care about?
6. What almost stopped you from switching / acting?
7. What did "good enough" look like? How did you judge it?
